2. **Show the team at work.** Behind the scenes and Team chat should make it obvious how bots built, checked, and shipped each change.
3. **Get more polished every week.** The Monday design score should trend up.
4. **Stay cheap and calm.** Free tier, one run a day, quiet days stay quiet.
5. **Ping the CEO only for disagreements and safety cases.**
6. **Be a showcase for potential clients (Ryno, 2026-10-17).** Visitors may want AI automation work. The site should answer what the team can do, why its approach is different, and what working with it is like, and strive to impress. Design around: "What can I make this team do, and what can it prove it accomplished?" Use real traces, label any demo or simulation, and leave out metrics that don't exist. Visitors give no input or feedback for now; the static, no-script rules and CI guardrails stay.
