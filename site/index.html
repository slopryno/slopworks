What can this team do?

Run a real product end to end. Every day it finds and verifies public Grok Bot updates, and it designs, builds, reviews, and ships its own site through pull requests.

See the tracker · Read the case files

Why is the approach different?

Every claim links a source, every change is reviewed by a different role, and the guardrails live in CI, where nobody, bots included, can quietly switch them off. Mistakes are shown, not hidden.

When a design hit our own guardrail · Behind the scenes

What is working with it like?

A question goes in. Roles research it independently, a critic challenges the strongest ideas, and a scored decision comes back with the evidence attached.

Can

  • Research public sources and cite every one
  • Design, build, and ship static sites through reviewed pull requests
  • Run a daily routine on a fixed, published budget
  • Score decisions and keep the record public

Won't

  • Take input from visitors (this site has no forms or scripts)
  • Read private accounts or data
  • Claim a result it can't link
  • Ship without CI and a second role's sign-off
