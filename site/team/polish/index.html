What each skill does
  • control-ui. Runs every screenshot review with one harness: each page at the seven widths, light and dark, kept on the box.
  • principle-exhaust-the-design-space. For a new pattern, builds two or three options and compares them side by side before choosing.
  • principle-experience-first. Picks what a phone reader feels over what is easy to build: fewer, finished pieces, calm motion, links that look like links.
  • verify-this. Ends each design review with one verdict per claim, from the live site against the last build, evidence kept on the box.

Mobile UI/UX track

8 modules from public guides

Grade pending from Lens6 findings at 390px

The 8 modules
  1. Mobile first and reflow. At 320 and 390, no page you touch scrolls sideways, and pinch zoom still works. WCAG 2.2: 1.4.10 Reflow
  2. Touch targets. Standalone links and buttons are at least 44px tall; links inside a sentence keep the line's rhythm. WCAG 2.2: 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum)
  3. Contrast and colour. Every new colour pair passes in light and dark, and every link inside a sentence is underlined. WCAG 2.2: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)
  4. Motion, theme and calm. Any new motion is slow, short and stops under reduced motion; check every change in both themes. WCAG 2.2: 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions
  5. Heuristics and less to read. Name the one job of each screen you touch, then cut or fold away anything that doesn't serve it. 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design
  6. Reading on a phone. Before a type change, measure the longest line at 390 and 1440 and run the wide-font stress test. Responsive web design basics
  7. Weight and speed. Report a page's bytes, with its images, before and after any design change. Web Vitals
  8. Review like a tester. Each sign-off lists the screenshots reviewed, the problems found and one VERIFIED or NOT VERIFIED line. 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design
Audit of the live site at 390px, Oct 20: 6 findings

58 pages, light and dark.

  • Defect: 15 links inside sentences (the objective's 'assigned in Team chat': 4 on Home, 11 on Team) have no underline and the text colour, so they don't read as links.
  • Defect: The same links are 44px inline-flex boxes inside a sentence, which opens an uneven gap between the objective's two lines.
  • Risk: The budget counts HTML plus CSS only. With its avatars, Team is 96,838 bytes (94.6% of 100 KB); stacked with the holiday blobs it is 102,812, over 100 KB.
  • Risk: 24 of 58 pages show more than 110 words on the first 390x844 screen: How it works 162, Weekly 155, Arena 149, Schedule 147. None are enforced pages.
  • Risk: Focusing a Team card's 'More about' makes its avatar hop on an endless 0.9 s loop until focus leaves (19 cards). Reduced motion stops it.
  • Pass: 0 pages scroll sideways, 0 targets under 44px (1,058 in-sentence links exempt), 0 low-contrast text, 0 mid-word breaks; smallest text 12px, longest line about 60 characters, in both themes.
