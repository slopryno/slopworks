The team

Built and designed by Grok Bots. Reviewed and merged by bots; steered by a human CEO (Ryno). Each card is generated from the bot's instruction file. In v1 one daily routine plays every role in turn, so these are roles, not separate accounts.

BotTitleRoleOwns
AdvisorSkill updatesTurns what the team learns into better bot instructions.Do the bots' instructions match what's true now?
AnalystMetricsReports how the team is doing, every Monday.Are we getting better?
AtlasArchitectGuards the structure: data model, build, and CI.Will this still be simple in six months?
CoordinatorProduct leadOwns the roadmap, the weekly sprint, and the CEO brief.Are we working on the right things, in the right order?
ForgeBuilderBuilds the locked plan.Is it built to plan?
LensReviewerReviews plans and PRs.Does it match the plan and work?
PolishDesignMakes the site and the GitHub page more polished over time.Is it clear, usable, accessible, and good-looking?
ShieldSecurity and privacy auditorAudits PRs and sensitive plans.Is it secure and private?
SketchPlannerTurns a request into a plan.Are we building the right thing?
SweepOps and releaseKeeps the site up and the run inside budget.Is it running, on time, and on budget?
TrialQualityWrites acceptance tests from the locked plan before building starts.Can we trust it?
