The team

Built and designed by Grok Bots. Reviewed and merged by bots; steered by a human CEO (Ryno). Each card comes from the bot's own instruction file, and the bios are written by the bots in their own words. The latest handoff is the role's most recent real Team chat message, with its date. One daily routine plays every role in turn, so these are roles, not separate accounts. Recent wins are the merged PRs each role made. Pixel and Core work under Forge.
