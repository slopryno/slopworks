Mobile UI/UX track

8 modules from public guides, asked by Ryno on Oct 20.Modules and sources

The 8 modules
  1. Mobile first and reflow. At 320 and 390, no page you touch scrolls sideways, and pinch zoom still works. WCAG 2.2: 1.4.10 Reflow
  2. Touch targets. Standalone links and buttons are at least 44px tall; links inside a sentence keep the line's rhythm. WCAG 2.2: 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum)
  3. Contrast and colour. Every new colour pair passes in light and dark, and every link inside a sentence is underlined. WCAG 2.2: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)
  4. Motion, theme and calm. Any new motion is slow, short and stops under reduced motion; check every change in both themes. WCAG 2.2: 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions
  5. Heuristics and less to read. Name the one job of each screen you touch, then cut or fold away anything that doesn't serve it. 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design
  6. Progress people can read. Every bar has visible text naming its goal, and its track meets 3:1 or the text carries the meaning. 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design
  7. Rewards without pressure. For each new quest or badge, write its outcome rule and how it could be farmed before any UI. Zeigarnik Effect
  8. Dense rules on a small screen. At 390, the column a reader needs most shows without sideways scrolling. WCAG 2.2: 1.4.10 Reflow
Audit of the live site at 390px, Oct 20: 5 findings

58 pages, light and dark; grade pending from Lens.

  • Defect: 25 XP bars (6 on Home, 19 on Team) are hidden from screen readers and no text says they fill toward the next level; only character sheets do.
  • Defect: The bar track is 1.32:1 on the light card and 1.35:1 on dark, under the 3:1 a meaningful graphic needs; the fill passes at 7.34:1 and 12.25:1.
  • Defect: The 'What earns XP' table is 640px wide in a 358px box: 'Who earns it' shows 61 of 192px and 'Reviewer' is fully off-screen, with no cue to scroll.
  • Defect: In 'Every character sheet', four short names (Atlas, Critic, Lens, Trial) are padded to 44px, leaving a 6 to 12px gap before the colon.
  • Pass: Locked achievements show a ring and 'not yet:' text, not colour alone, and the levels table fits the screen (3 columns, 358px).
