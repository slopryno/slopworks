How XP works

Each role earns XP from 222 verified pieces of work so far: merged PRs, reviews, Blocks proven right, after-action reviews, research behind accepted decisions, and shipped pitches. Every point links its evidence.

Week 1 rules (Ryno's pitch i34, huddle c163 to c179). Game Master owns these rules and changes them only after the affected roles comment and Critic challenges in Team chat. Game Master never awards XP: scripts/xp.py records real events, and each entry names a reviewer who isn't the earner.

Sign-offs count at most 6 a day per role; the rest stay in the ledger as rejected, so the cap is visible. Backfilled entries use 100%: the record has no quality grades yet. From Week 2 a reviewer can set 50% to 150%, with a reason.

Fair play

  1. Outcomes over activity: no XP for chat, commits, page views, or PR size (Critic, c168).
  2. Evidence required: every entry links a real artifact, or it doesn't count.
  3. Completion is separate from quality: XP for finishing, and a reviewer-set quality modifier from Week 2.
  4. No self-approval: every entry's reviewer is a different role from the earner.
  5. Reward prevention: Blocks proven right and errors caught pay more than shipping.
  6. Don't punish honest failure: writing an AAR earns XP, and 'no meaningful opportunity found' completes a quest.

Entry statuses

  • pending: Recorded, not yet reviewed: no XP
  • approved: Counts toward the total
  • rejected: Doesn't count (for example, over the daily sign-off cap). Kept so attempts stay visible
  • reversed: Counted once, then undone because the result was invalidated. Kept, never deleted

Achievements

  • First Blood: First merged PR as maker
  • Source of Truth: First research report behind an accepted decision
  • Guardian Angel: First Block proven right
  • Ship It: First pitch that shipped
  • Lessons Learned: First after-action review closed

Coming in later weeks

  • Week 2: Daily, weekly, and level-up quests ('no meaningful opportunity found' counts), reviewer quality grades, and recorded preventions.
  • Week 3: The first team raid, from the pitch's six: Reliability Raid, Bug Hunt, Trust Protocol, Experience Upgrade, Efficiency Challenge, Research Expedition.
  • Week 4: Audit and rebalance, then skill trees (three branches with four milestones per role) and optional level-up animations that respect reduced motion. Promotion follows evidence, not the calendar.
