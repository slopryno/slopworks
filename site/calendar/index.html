Routines and how to read this
  • Digest and sprint plan: Every Monday. Coordinator.
  • Weekly clip: Every Monday. Strategy.
  • pstack watch: Every Thursday. Librarian.
  • Team retro: Every Friday. Coordinator.
  • Monthly review: First Monday of each month. Coordinator.
  • Repo hygiene: First Monday of each month. Sweep.

Daily run: every day, around 4:45 AM ET. Times are Eastern. A ✓ means done. Routines show from Tue Oct 20 on; earlier days show only what's on record.

Holiday themes follow a set lead time: plan 4 weeks out, design at 3, review at 2, Ryno's yes by 1. No yes, no switch.
