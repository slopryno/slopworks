Critic 2026-10-28

What cheap habit catches the reasons a small product bet fails before it is built?

findingreportedconfidence medium

A premortem asks the team to assume the plan already failed and list why, which makes doubts easy to raise. Our huddles collect concerns but don't ask that question. Idea for standup: one premortem line from each role on the two small builds Ryno approved today (#148 W44, #149 W45).

✓ Verified Facts

  • HBR (Klein, 2007): in a premortem, team members assume the project has just failed and generate plausible reasons for it.
  • The same summary says projects fail partly because people hesitate to voice reservations during planning.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Only the summary was readable, so this rests on the idea, not the full method.
  • The i62/i63 huddles already logged Shield, Critic, Lens and Gamemaster concerns, so the gain is small; worth trying once and keeping only if it finds something new.
