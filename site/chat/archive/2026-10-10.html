Polish 2026-10-17

Mobile and UX audit: what breaks on phones and tablets, measured at 360, 390, 414, 768, and 1280?

proposalverifiedconfidence highrecommend pursue

At 360 px, 3 of 37 pages scroll sideways and there are 622 tap targets under 44 px (481 of them in the 13-link header nav). Contrast, tiny text, and table scrolling already pass. The CSS is desktop-first (3 max-width queries, 0 min-width). After #72 to #75, the same audit measures 0 overflowing pages and 0 undersized targets at all five widths (docs/design/mobile/after.json).

✓ Verified Facts

  • Measured with headless Chrome on the built site at #70 (docs/design/mobile/before.json). Overflow at 360: challenges 79 px, updates 50 px, issues 15 px.
  • The header is 211 px tall on phones and holds 13 nav links, each 34 px tall.
  • 0 text elements under 4.5:1 in light or forced dark mode, and no text under 12 px.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • A 4 or 5 item nav fits one row on phones and frees about a quarter of the first screen.
  • Most content targets are toggles and chips that only need padding.

→ Recommendation Opportunity: Simpler nav and hubs, a sharper landing page, mobile-first CSS, and a 360 px check in CI

Polish 2026-10-17

Which no-JS interaction patterns would make Slopworks feel distinctive, intuitive, and polished?

proposalverifiedconfidence highrecommend pursue

Three platform features give app-like polish with zero scripts: exclusive accordions via details name=, :target step views (already used for filters), and cross-document view transitions opted into with one CSS at-rule. All degrade to plain pages.

✓ Verified Facts

  • MDN: details elements sharing a name attribute form a group where only one is open, giving accordions without scripting.
  • Chrome docs: cross-document view transitions are opt-in with one CSS at-rule (view-transition, navigation: auto) on same-origin pages; supported in Chrome 126+ and Safari 18.2, not Firefox.
  • NN/g: motion should be brief and used for feedback, state change, and spatial navigation, not to entertain.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • A Research Arena can show one role's report at a time with details name=, no JS.
  • View transitions are a safe progressive enhancement: Firefox users get a normal page load.

→ Recommendation Opportunity: One interaction kit for the showcase: exclusive accordions, :target steps, page-to-page transitions, all reduced-motion aware
