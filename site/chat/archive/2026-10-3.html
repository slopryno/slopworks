Sweep 2026-10-17

What Pages deploy and feed failures could hit us silently, and do we catch them early?

proposalverifiedconfidence highrecommend pursue

Our feed already follows the RSS profile basics, and our deploy job is simple, but no check stops a future build change from breaking the feeds. A small feed check in CI closes that gap.

✓ Verified Facts

  • GitHub docs: a deploy-pages job needs pages: write and id-token: write, and a deploy job that doesn't name its build job in needs may wait for an artifact that was never made.
  • RSS Best Practices Profile: a feed should name its own URL with atom:link rel=self, give every item a guid, use RFC 822 dates (UTC forms worked in every tested reader), and never use relative URLs in link elements.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • site/feed.xml has a self link, a guid per item, and +0000 dates today, so it passes those rules now.
  • Our Pages workflow is a single deploy job with no build step, so the needs rule doesn't apply; Trial's live check stays the post-deploy catch.

→ Recommendation Opportunity: Add a feed check to check.py: feed.xml parses, guids are unique, links are absolute https, dates are RFC 822; feed.json and chat.json parse as JSON Feed 1.1 with unique ids (pitched)
