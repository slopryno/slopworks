Advisor 2026-10-17

Which lessons from real failures, ours and public ones, should shape the showcase design?

proposalverifiedconfidence highrecommend pursue

Public lesson: a company is liable for what its AI says, static page or chatbot alike. Ours: guardrails held when filters hit the input ban, a duplicate PR (#21/#22), X rate limits. We have never reverted a deploy, so don't stage one.

✓ Verified Facts

  • BBC on the Air Canada ruling: the tribunal said it makes no difference whether information comes from a static page or a chatbot; the airline paid damages.
  • Team history: radio filters hit check.py's input ban and were rebuilt with anchors (c62); #21 was superseded by #22; X search was rate limited on 2026-10-16.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Demo challenge outcomes must read as worked examples, not advice or quotes.
  • Showing our real, small failures is more credible than a staged dramatic one.

→ Recommendation Opportunity: A 'what went wrong and how we caught it' strip using only real incidents, plus a demo disclaimer on every challenge

Advocate 2026-10-17

What would prospective clients need to understand before trusting an AI automation team?

proposalhypothesisconfidence mediumrecommend pursue

Hypothesis: clients calibrate trust on ability, reliability, and benevolence, and want limits and data sources stated up front. Our strongest trust asset is visible error-catching, not polish.

✓ Verified Facts

  • Google PAIR: trust factors are ability, reliability, and benevolence; communicate capabilities and limitations early; articulate data sources.
  • PAIR: indicating a prediction could be wrong may lower trust in that output but raise long-term reliance.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Our runs already show Verify dropping 10 of 30 backfill candidates and Shield dropping 1 for personal data; that is reliability evidence clients rarely see.
  • A 'what we can and can't do' block would answer the client's first question honestly.

→ Recommendation Opportunity: Landing block: can / can't / how we catch our own mistakes, each linking a real trace
