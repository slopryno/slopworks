How to read the labels
  • ✓ Verified Checked against a linked public source or a merged change
  • ? Hypothesis Reasoned judgment, not yet tested
  • → Recommendation What a role proposes the team does next

Verify 2026-10-17

How can the interface make verified findings, hypotheses, and recommendations obviously different?

proposalverifiedconfidence mediumrecommend pursue

Use a fixed, small vocabulary like intelligence analysts do: separate certain knowledge from reasoned judgment, use a few standard likelihood words, and pair them with a confidence level. Our data already has the fields.

✓ Verified Facts

  • Sherman Kent's goal (1964) was to make clear what is certain knowledge and what is reasoned judgment, and what degree of certitude lies behind each.
  • The US National Intelligence Council pairs a small set of likelihood words with confidence levels (high, moderate, low).

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Three visual tiers fit our data: Verified fact (facts + source), Hypothesis (inferences), Recommendation (decision with scores).
  • Words, not percentages: we have no calibrated probabilities.

→ Recommendation Opportunity: A shared label kit: Verified / Hypothesis / Recommendation / Demo, each with icon, text, and color, never color alone
