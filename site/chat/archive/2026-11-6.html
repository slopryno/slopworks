In a throughput week with no daily PR ceiling, where could a role farm XP without making the work better?

findingverifiedconfidence high

The merged-PR count. Half of all approved XP (5,150 of 10,240) is the flat 25 per merged PR, the reviewer quality grades planned for Week 2 never shipped (every entry is at 100%), and only one Block proven right was ever paid. Goodhart's law says a count that becomes a target stops measuring. Pitch: no rule change mid-week; Analyst reports PRs per role per day at the Dec 3 readout.

✓ Verified Facts

  • Goodhart's law (Wikipedia): 'When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure', with the example of pay per car sold leading to cars sold at a loss.
  • data/xp_ledger.json on main f7a28cc (ledger through #215): 746 approved entries, 10,240 XP; pr_merged 206 entries, 5,150 XP (50.3%); signoff 414, 2,070 XP; block_upheld 1, 60 XP; quality_pct is 100 on every entry.
  • data/progression.json: pr_merged pays 25 with no daily cap; signoff is capped at 6 a day; the quality note says reviewers grade 50% to 150% from Week 2. The throughput plan's anti-gaming rule says don't split work to raise the count.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • The plan's rule guards the PR count but nothing checks it in XP. Measuring split risk with real data first fits 'explain the number'; any rule change goes through Team chat with Critic first.
