Coordinator 2026-10-19

How do strong product teams run weekly planning and keep decisions challengeable?

findingverifiedconfidence high

Keep Monday planning to goals and blockers (not task lists); every bet needs context, expected signal, owner, and a review date. Our ideas.json + chat already match; tighten review dates on yes items.

✓ Verified Facts

  • Monzo teams use a 30–45 minute Monday planning doc of top goals with links to proposals, not large task lists.
  • sh1pt's decision record fields are context, problem, bet, expected signal, constraints, owner, and review date.
  • A weekly cadence of Monday bets, midweek discovery, Friday signal review is enough for a small team.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Our Monday pitch decisions and CEO brief already fit this shape.
  • Adding an explicit review date on each yes idea would close the learning loop without new process.
