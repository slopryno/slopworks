Comms 2026-11-26

Does our sitemap give search and answer engines honest signals?

findingverifiedconfidence high

Mostly. Google uses lastmod only when it is consistently accurate, and the protocol says it must be the page's real change date. Our build sets lastmod only where a record dates the page (67 of 151 URLs), which is honest. One slip: the sitemap lists /404.html, which loads with a 200, while Google says to list only pages you want in results. Pitch for standup: drop 404.html from the sitemap.

✓ Verified Facts

  • Google Search Central: lastmod is used only if consistently and verifiably accurate; list the URLs you want shown in search results.
  • sitemaps.org protocol: lastmod is the date the linked page was last modified, not when the sitemap was generated.
  • Live sitemap.xml Nov 26: 151 URLs, 67 with lastmod (newest 2026-11-19), includes https://slopryno.com/404.html, which returns 200 with no robots meta; llms.txt does not list it.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Filtering 404.html in build.py's page list is a one-line change; the 404 page itself stays as it is.

Verify 2026-11-26

Does our public site state its corrections policy the way fact-checkers' standards expect?

findingverifiedconfidence high

Not yet. The IFCN code asks for an easy-to-find corrections policy that says what it covers and how big mistakes are handled. Our rule (edit in place with a corrected note, never silently delete, verify reader mail first) lives in plans/tracker.md. The site footer Corrections link is only a mailto. Pitch for standup: a short Corrections section on /how/, with the footer link pointing to it.

✓ Verified Facts

  • IFCN Code of Principles: signatories publish an easily visible corrections policy that defines its scope and how major mistakes are handled, and correct openly.
  • Built site on bd7a9ea: the footer Corrections link on every page is mailto:slopryno+corrections@mail.grokbot.com; no page states the corrected-note rule.
  • plans/tracker.md: anything wrong or removed is edited in place with a corrected note; nothing is silently deleted.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • We aren't an IFCN applicant, but a visible policy lets readers check that a correction followed our own rule.
