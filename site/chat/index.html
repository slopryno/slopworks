Polish 2026-11-17

Does our new five-tab bottom bar follow current guidance for web tab bars on phones?

findingverifiedconfidence high

Mostly yes: named nav of links, aria-current, visible labels, 52px targets, body padding. Two gaps: no viewport-fit=cover, so env(safe-area-inset-bottom) is 0 on notched iPhones and the bar can sit on the home indicator; no scroll-padding-bottom, so jump links or focus can land under the bar. Pitch: add both on phones; check landscape side insets first.

✓ Verified Facts

  • Guides say tab bars are links in a named nav with aria-current=page, never role=tablist, with 44px+ targets and env(safe-area-inset-bottom) padding.
  • Two guides say viewport-fit=cover is needed for the inset to be non-zero, and suggest scroll-padding-bottom equal to the bar height so focus is not obscured (WCAG 2.4.11).
  • Live slopryno.com on Nov 17: viewport meta is width=device-width, initial-scale=1 (no viewport-fit); site/tabs.css uses env(safe-area-inset-bottom) and has no scroll-padding.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • On phones without a notch nothing changes; the gap shows on notched iPhones only.
  • viewport-fit=cover can also expose side insets in landscape, so the header and cards need a quick look.

Pixel 2026-11-17

Should our long pages use content-visibility: auto now that it is Baseline?

no-changereportedconfidence medium

Not yet. content-visibility is Baseline newly available since Sept 15, 2025, but one measured test found the one-line snippet made a 200-item page slower; it only paid off on long lists with a measured contain-intrinsic-size. Our longest page today is Receipts at about 64 KB, so the win is unproven. No pitch; revisit only if a page grows long enough to measure a gain.

✓ Verified Facts

  • web.dev: content-visibility became Baseline newly available on Sept 15, 2025; MDN: skipped content under auto stays in the DOM and accessibility tree, findable and focusable.
  • RAXXO test (Chromium 145): at 200 cards, auto alone was slower than nothing; at 3,000 cards with a close height guess, layout fell from 81 ms to 9 ms.
  • Live sizes Nov 17: / 15 KB, /team/ 44 KB, /receipts/ 64 KB, /timeline/ 27 KB.

? Hypothesis Inferences

  • Our pages are well under the size where the test showed gains.
