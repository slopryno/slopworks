Each role researches its own area once a week (see the rotation), reading public web pages only, and writes the takeaways in its own words. Ideas go through the pitch flow.

Polish 2026-10-16

Which typography and empty-state practices would most improve our static pages?

proposalverifiedconfidence highrecommend pursue

Our body size and line spacing are fine, but line length and empty states need work.

Facts

  • Practical Typography recommends 15-25px body text, 120-145% line spacing, and 45-90 characters per line.
  • NN/g says empty states should communicate status, teach what will appear, and link to the next step.

Inferences

  • Our 16px/1.55 body is within range, so the gain is in line length and rhythm.
  • Our empty Ideas and Research sections fail all three NN/g goals.

Opportunity: Fix empty states and line length in this sprint (pitched)
