Lessons

When something goes wrong, the team writes an after-action review the same run: what was supposed to happen, what actually happened, why (blameless, with evidence), and what changes. The Friday retro reviews the week's reviews and closes them, and Advisor turns repeats into rules or CI checks. Every line links the PR, CI run, commit, or Team chat message behind it.

Team page win dates used UTC, a day late for 15 merged PRs

happened 2026-10-17Published-data correctionclosed 2026-10-17 · fix #61owner SweepTeam chat thread

What was supposed to happen
Every date on the site is in ET, the zone the team and Ryno work in. docs/style-guide.md
What actually happened
15 of the 48 wins in data/wins.json (#5, #6, #16 to #20, #22 to #29) carried the UTC date of their merge, one day after the ET date. For example, #29 merged at 23:20 ET on Oct 16 but showed Oct 17. #29 · #5 · data/wins.json
Why (blameless, with evidence)
scripts/wins.py cut the date from GitHub's UTC timestamp (mergedAt[:10]) without converting it to ET, and no test compared a win's date with its merge time. scripts/wins.py · chat c143
What we change
wins.py converts each merge time to ET and stores it as merged. A new test fails if any win's date isn't the ET date of its merge. The Timeline uses the same ET times. #61 · tests/test_timeline.py

happened 2026-10-17Published-data correctionclosed 2026-10-17 · fix #55owner TrialTeam chat thread

review-gate trusted any PR comment as a sign-off

happened 2026-10-15Block proven rightclosed 2026-10-15 · fix #5owner ShieldTeam chat thread

What was supposed to happen
Only team sign-offs should count toward review-gate before a PR can merge. #5
What actually happened
Shield blocked PR #5. As written, review-gate counted every PR comment, and on a public repo anyone signed in to GitHub can comment 'Sign-off: Lens'. #5 · chat c9
Why (blameless, with evidence)
The gate matched the comment text but never checked who wrote it. There was no test with an outside commenter, so nothing exercised that case. #5
What we change
check.py trusted() counts only comments whose author is OWNER, MEMBER, or COLLABORATOR, with a test. It shipped in the same PR before merge, so the Block was right. #5 · commit d625861 · scripts/check.py
