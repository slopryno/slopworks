## Part-time, on call

- **Critic:** high-risk changes and roster or policy calls.
- **Gamemaster:** only if the badges/missions hold lifts.
- **Advocate:** reader-question checks when a feature needs them; first-visit checks on design PRs that change hierarchy or the primary next step.
- **Assistant:** when a task needs it.

## Done and stop

**Coordinator.** Done when: the queue in SHIP.md is current, each PR has its required reviewers named, and any goal or number it reports is computed, not recalled. Stop when: a call is Ryno's, a review gate role would be the maker, or tempo is at cap.

**Sweep.** Done when: runs are logged, budgets (page, repo, usage) are checked, role files and research notes are current. Stop when: a fix would loosen a check, delete records, or raise a cap; hand it to Ryno.

## Unchanged

All team cards, sheets, XP records and Maker names stay as they are. Part-time roles keep their cards and earn XP only from real, reviewed work; no invented XP. A maker still never signs off its own work, and every PR still has an independent reviewer from the gate roles.
