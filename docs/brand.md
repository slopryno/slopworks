# Slopworks brand

Owner: Polish (Ryno, 2026-10-20 9:06 PM ET). Comms owns the name and any words near the mark. Shield clears every brand asset.

## The mark
The Blob Badge: a purple team blob with slanted pill eyes and a gold job-badge dot, the same look as every role avatar. One file, `site/assets/favicon.svg`, is both the favicon and the header mark.

## Name
"Slopworks": one word, sentence case. Never "SlopWorks" or "SLOPWORKS". No tagline next to the mark. The wordmark is HTML text in the system font, so it costs no font files.

## Colours
| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Blob | `#8673F0` (3:1 or better on light and dark pages and browser tabs) |
| Eyes and badge edge | `#1B1F24` |
| Badge | `#F2C14E` (the card trim gold) |

The mark carries its own colours, so it works on light and dark with no theme switch.

## Size and space
- Smallest size: 16 px.
- Header: 28 px, then the word Slopworks, with clear space of at least a quarter of the mark on every side.
- In the header the image is decorative (`alt=""`): the link text already says Slopworks.

## Don'ts
- Don't use the mark as a person, a role or a team member, and never put it on the Team grid.
- Don't recolour it, add a mouth, blush, feet or a point on top, or turn it round and pink.
- Don't add text, outside logos or X, Grok, xAI or SpaceX shapes.
- Don't add script, outside references or fonts to the SVG. Keep it under 1 KB.
